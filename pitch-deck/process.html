Clear process / short feedback loops

Know what happens next.

Focused work is easier to trust when the scope, feedback, and handoff stay visible from day one.

01 / ALIGN

Confirm the outcome.

Define the priority, page set, content, access, and what “done” means before work begins.

Written scope
02 / BUILD

Make the work visible.

Implement the agreed experience with async updates and a live staging link for review.

Working preview
03 / REFINE

Close the important gaps.

Complete responsive QA, the agreed revisions, and final checks against the original outcome.

Focused feedback
04 / HANDOFF

Leave with ownership.

Launch or transfer the work with technical notes, documentation, and the next sensible step.

Clear handoff
