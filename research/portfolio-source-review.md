# Portfolio Source Review

## Aethelon

The deployed Aethelon project presents a luxury-furniture ecommerce concept. Its publicly visible navigation includes shopping, campaigns, categories, advanced search, a try-on experience, a vault, and a blog. The landing page visibly features a canvas-based hero, curated room collections, and calls to action for an AI-powered room visualizer and an AI assistant. Portfolio copy should describe this as a concept or product exploration unless the owner separately confirms commercial launch and client context.

## Novexa

The deployed Novexa project identifies itself as a concept exploration, not a live product. The visible experience combines a canvas-led shoe hero, collection commerce UI, product imagery, a demo AI personal stylist, and email capture. Portfolio copy must not repeat its promotional claims, media logos, reviews, performance statistics, or pricing as substantiated outcomes; those are on-site concept content rather than independent proof.

## Asset Selection Principle

Use only images and captures that are already present in the project repositories or deployed experiences. Attribute the work as self-directed concept/product development, avoid client identification, and do not state conversion, revenue, adoption, performance, or testimonial results unless the user provides verifiable evidence.
